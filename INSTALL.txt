DRIVER COMMAND 3.6.0 — INSTALL

NEW INSTALL
1. Extract the complete folder.
2. In the extracted folder, run:  python3 -m http.server 8080
3. Open:  http://localhost:8080

You may open index.html directly for ordinary dashboard use. Installation and offline service-worker caching require HTTP or HTTPS.

UPDATING AN EXISTING COPY
- Open Settings & data and create a Full backup first.
- Replace the old application files with this complete folder.
- Reload the page. An installed home-screen copy may need to be fully closed and reopened after the deployment loads.
- Existing version-3 browser data remains compatible.
- Confirm that your old shifts are present before deleting the backup.

3.6 MONEY PLAN
- Future shifts use 5% of gross earnings for the vehicle fund.
- Future shifts use 20% of gross earnings for investments.
- There is no savings allocation.
- The 20% investment contribution is split into Bitcoin 40%, Solana 30%, SCHG 20%, and AAVE 10%.
- Take out / move = recorded gas + the full vehicle and investment allocation.
- Open Settings & data → Your money plan → Edit plan to change the percentages later.
- Existing shifts keep the allocation plan they originally used.

SHIFT FLOW
- Start shift asks for starting mileage first.
- Pause shift stops work time during lunch or errands.
- End shift asks for ending mileage, earnings, and expenses.
- Finish & show money plan displays the exact amounts to move into the vehicle fund and each investment.
